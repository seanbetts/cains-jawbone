# Approved historical discovery corpus

This is a source-admission policy, not clue evidence. The workflow overhaul authorizes bibliographic checking and admission of eligible editions within the repositories below. A fresh run starts with an empty `Sources/catalog.json`; it may add individual works after reading edition metadata. No previous run's source matches or interpretations are supplied.

- Project Gutenberg: https://www.gutenberg.org — historical works; distinguish original publication/edition from ebook release date and modern introductions.
- Wikisource: https://wikisource.org — historical primary texts and period reference works; distinguish original text from modern annotations.
- Google Books: https://books.google.com — scans of editions published by 1934; read title/imprint pages and cited passages, not search snippets alone.
- Internet Archive: https://archive.org — dated scans of historical editions; check the item, edition and actual scan rather than accepting upload metadata as a publication date.

Useful categories include period dictionaries, almanacs, bibliographies, original poetry/prose/drama, travel guides, natural history and contemporary timetables. The category is not a clue identification. Verify each selected edition and record `title`, `year`, `url`, `admission_note`, stable S ID and revision before treating a passage as evidence.

Sources outside this approved discovery corpus require explicit admission authorization. Undated works, modern wordplay handbooks, modern puzzle analyses, solution pages and modern search-result summaries are not admitted evidence. Modern hosting of historical content is permitted; modern interpretation is excluded. If an eligible text cannot be inspected, record the access limitation and defer the lookup rather than inventing a citation.
