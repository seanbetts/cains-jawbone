# Wordplay reference and view

A detector candidate under page Notes uses:

- **Evidence:** V ID and exact short span.
- **Mechanism and operation:** exact input, transformation and output; normalization stated.
- **Literal alternative:** what the wording ordinarily means.
- **Contextual motivation:** why this span warranted this detector.
- **Support:** tentative, with a reason distinguishing mechanical validity from intention.
- **Falsifier and next test:** specific evidence that would decide the reading; R ID if lookup needed.

These are candidate notes, not accepted claims. Do not copy this empty reference as a live index entry. No convincing candidate is a valid finding.

A synthesis view links the canonical **C ID**, **V IDs**, **R IDs if relevant**, **interpretation**, **support status**, and **remaining alternative/next test**. State why it might matter without silently promoting a date/place/narrator/order consequence. Such consequences require separate typed claims under the schema.
