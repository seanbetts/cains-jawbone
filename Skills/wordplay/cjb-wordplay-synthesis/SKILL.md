# Wordplay synthesis

Apply phase permissions from the playbook and the record contract in `Templates/SCHEMA.md`. Phase 1 captures detector candidates only. Later synthesis may select zero candidates; there is no minimum or fixed quota.

1. Read each original exact span in context and the detector's operation. Collapse overlapping descriptions of the same transformation; they are one observation, not independent support.
2. Check mechanical validity separately from whether the author likely intended it. Explain local indicators, contextual payoff and historical plausibility; preserve the literal alternative. Recurrence by itself does not establish intentional wordplay.
3. Reject transformations requiring unsupported letters, pronunciation, arbitrary boundaries or modern senses. Suspend otherwise valid readings with no discriminating evidence. A familiar name as output is not confirmation.
4. For a worthwhile interpretation, write a typed wordplay C claim with V IDs, alternatives, falsifier, support reason and next test. Use `Templates/wordplay.md` for the readable view and link it from the wordplay index; do not duplicate rationale as corroboration.
5. A candidate quotation/place/date creates a bounded R question. Verify source passages before attribution. Narrator, identity, date-in-story or ordering consequences each require their own permitted claims and dependency revisions. Synthesis never supplies a final ordering.

If nothing explains the passage better than its literal reading, write “No convincing wordplay” or leave the synthesis view empty. Record what would reopen a suspended candidate; avoid broad rescanning without a specific dispute.
