# Page extraction

Use the phase playbook for permissions and `Templates/SCHEMA.md` for records. Read the complete page before extracting minimal spans. Keep the literal reading alongside possible allusion or wordplay; mark narration, dialogue, quotation or unclear layer. Never edit above Notes.

1. Record exact V evidence spans and offsets for substantive observations: explicit entities, relationships, temporal language, place, objects, symptoms, typography and voice. A reference to a historical death is not an in-world event. Do not silently resolve pronouns or aliases.
2. Write short Notes grouped only where useful: observations and V IDs; literal alternatives; candidate references/wordplay; unknowns and R IDs. A page may have no convincing wordplay. Unusual diction is a page signature, not a new narrator identity.
3. Make one anomaly scan. Call only detectors motivated by a local indicator, marked wording or meaningful ambiguity; do not run all ten automatically. In Phase 1, capture their candidate blocks only.
4. Queue small source questions with the uncertainty they could decide; do not research during the first full pass. Explicit relationship and means observations are allowed without accusations.
5. Update readable indices with IDs, not duplicated reasoning. Mark the page in coverage after Notes review. Later rereads add or withdraw evidence and review dependent claims rather than erasing inconvenient observations.

Choose manageable batches based on attention and complexity; there is no candidate quota. Finish the full initial coverage gate before external resolution or cross-page synthesis. Run body and state verification after each edited batch.
