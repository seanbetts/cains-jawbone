# Means and methods

Apply the phase matrix. Extract factual observations in any phase; accusations require later permitted claims. Record exact spans for substances/objects as written, preparation/access, symptoms, timing and who is present. Distinguish an administered substance from a metaphor, ordinary meal, quoted description or unknown object.

Link observations into the motifs view. Preserve benign explanations and uncertainty; access does not establish administration, poison identity, intent or a death. Queue a narrowly scoped historical lookup only if it could decide an ambiguity. Research follows corpus admission and extraction-first policy; do not supply invented toxicological specifics.

If context supports possible in-world harm, flag it for permitted murder analysis. Do not count a symptom or mention of a weapon as an event. Keep means claims subordinate to exact evidence and separate from victim/perpetrator identity; changes to one must not silently certify the others.
