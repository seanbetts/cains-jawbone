# Clusters and typed ordering claims

Read the phase matrix and canonical schema. Phase 3 forms groups only. In Phase 4, propose internal constraints; global stitching belongs to Phase 5+. Preserve a partial order and unknown assignments. No permutation search, arbitrary cluster counts or lexical-score optimization.

For each proposed relation, write one C claim with exact V evidence, current premise revisions, alternatives, falsifier and next test. Distinguish same-narrator, same-entity, same-scene and shared-reference from directional precedence or immediate adjacency. An ordered quotation may support a continuation, but its context must establish why it is placed there; common quotation choice need not imply one speaker or scene.

A cluster card in the readable hypotheses view lists pages (including overlap), strongest specific observations and provenance, quotation-layer versus narrator-layer features, exclusion tells, unassigned/contested pages and the test that would split/merge it. Do not label predecessor/successor endpoints during clusters-only work.

For ordering, examine exact page endings/beginnings, syntax, conversation, relative time, physical movement, object states and narrative consequences. Keep event date versus narration date and flashback versus present action distinct. One decisive continuation can outweigh several generic similarities; justify specificity, independence and alternatives rather than counting dimensions.

Every proposed boundary links an adjacency claim or remains unresolved. A zero lexical match is neither a defect nor proof of a narrator transition; shared whole-page vocabulary cannot establish direction. The concordance finds passages to reread, not acceptable joins.

For a proposed revision, name affected constraints and test all changed surrounding boundaries. Adopt only because evidence favors those relationships over live alternatives without unresolved contradictions. Do not adopt a rearrangement because lexical counts improve. Test with `cjb-falsification`, update dependency revisions, and validate the partial/complete order. Completion alone never raises support.
