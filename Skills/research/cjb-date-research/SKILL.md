# Date research

Use `cjb-quote-research` for source admission, searching and truthful dispositions; the phase playbook controls when research is allowed. Consult the admitted edition of Chambers’ Book of Days when relevant, not an unspecified presumed edition.

Distinguish source-entry date, historical event date, story event date and narrator's time of telling. Record the volume/page and actual passage read. List alternative referents, variant calendars or feast-day conventions if they matter. A historical anniversary can identify a calendar day without establishing the story's year, narrator or neighboring page.

Record exact/context/variant support in the R record. Only the stated question can be verified; a proposed story date or precedence requires its own permitted C claim and current dependencies. If no match is found, list the corpus and variants checked and retain no_match; do not assert that a name-day is fictional. Stop bounded unproductive searches under the falsification policy.
