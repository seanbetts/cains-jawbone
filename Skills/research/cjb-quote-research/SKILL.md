# Quotation and source research

Read the phase playbook for extraction-first permissions and source admission; use the canonical schema for research outcomes. In Phase 1, capture only a minimal exact V span, literal reading and small R question. Do not guess a source attribution from familiarity or remembered solutions.

When resolution is permitted, choose a recorded question and identify what an answer would change. Search only admitted historical works. Record actual searches and variants; open the cited passage, check edition/date and source location, and distinguish exact, variant and merely contextual matches. Explain variant wording rather than silently correcting the puzzle.

Set `verified` only if the entire stated question is answered by passages actually read with precise locations and support. A snippet gives a candidate. A partial answer remains partial or splits into smaller questions. A bounded unsuccessful search is no_match, not proof of original authorship or fictionality. Deferred work includes a reason and reopening condition.

Write the result once in State/research, with S ID, pinned source revision, location, match, verification and supports fields; link it from Notes and the quotes view. Source identity is separate from narrator identity, scene date or ordering. A derived interpretation needs its own typed C claim, source-result explanation and C/R/S revision dependencies. Increment research/source revision when its result or admitted edition metadata changes, and suspend/reassess downstream consumers of old revisions. Verified research must pin each current catalog source revision. Reassess the cited passage before updating a changed source pin and increment the research revision; stale verified citations cannot support live claims. Do not promote support for thematic resemblance into exact source identification.
