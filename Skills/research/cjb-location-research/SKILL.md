# Location research

Use `cjb-quote-research` for admission, citations and research statuses; the phase playbook controls permission. Consult an admitted, dated Highways & Byways volume or another explicitly approved historical work relevant to the question. A series name without edition metadata is not sufficient admission.

Compare specific physical/topographic features and exact phrasing, then list plausible alternatives. Read the cited page and record volume/year/location, match type and the part of the question supported. Distinguish a real place's identity from proof that the narrator is there, a feasible route from a traveled route, and description-time from journey-time.

Record a candidate or partial match honestly; general resemblance is not an exact identification. Route/ordering consequences require separate permitted C claims with dependencies. For no_match, record checked regions/volumes/variants and next discriminating evidence; do not infer a fictional locale from a failed search.
